# Frequently Asked Questions

Following responses are written by me, FlyingMeta. None of these should be read like opinions of any other contributor, please keep that in mind!

## Is the PC Port compatible with the Animal Crossing Deluxe mod by Cuyler?

Not yet. A Deluxe port is planned by Cuyler in the future. I would recommend keeping an eye on it in [Animal Crossing Modding Server](https://discord.gg/HcpymVA).

## Will there be mod support?

Yes, it is planned for v1.1.

## Will there be online play?

Haven't decided yet. If it happens, it will be at the earliest after the modding API is released.

## Is this vibecoded?

No. AI couldn't write a functional PC port by itself when this port was released. It was and still is AI assisted development.

## Why is the initial version one big commit?

This started out as a hobby project I had no intention of sharing with anyone, so my local repository contains assets from the game. 
I made a new repository just to upload it to Github. Sharing the original could result in this project getting taken down.

## Why did you switch to Aurora?

As much as I'm proud of what I've managed to do with my own implementation, [Aurora](https://github.com/encounter/aurora) is better written, more performant and provides more options for us to 
improve the port. Frankly, without Aurora I had no intention to even attempt to support macOS/iOS and Android. My OpenGL implementation is still available under the
legacy branch, for anyone who needs it to possibly port it to older devices.

## Why did you change the name of the port/repository to Nookery?

When I shared this repo with people, I did so in the modding server without mentioning it anywhere else. I frankly didn't expect it to get this much attention. 
At the time I was already tired, and just wanted to share it with the people on the server, so they could playtest it. I just picked the most simple possible option.
It's obviously not a great or very creative name, so changing it seemed the best way to move forward. We also support android and iOS at the moment, would be weird to call it 
a PC port, lol.
